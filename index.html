XPERT · fantasy sports engine

A personal recommendation engine for Fantasy Premier League, UCL Fantasy and F1 Fantasy. It combines underlying stats from every competition into expected points per gameweek, then picks optimal transfers, captains and chip timing.

  • Modelling: team ratings from npxG, player xG/xA rates, minutes projection, injury risk from acute:chronic workload, and return-to-form curves
  • Optimiser: mixed-integer program (HiGHS) for squad, XI and captain over a multi-gameweek horizon, including −4 hits
  • Chip simulator: Wildcard, Free Hit, Bench Boost and Triple Captain tested in every gameweek, adjusted for mini-league rivals
  • Insights: colour-blind-safe charts with auto-written takeaways, manager tactical profiles and a 10-GW planner
  • Data: official FPL API, Understat, FotMob and ESPN, self-hosted on an Oracle Cloud VM behind Caddy
